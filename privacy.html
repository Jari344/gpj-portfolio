    Privacy

    Short version: this site doesn’t collect your data. Here is the detail.

    Who runs this site

    This is the portfolio of Jari Abbas Rizvi, from Betul, India. You can reach me through the contact page.

    What this site collects

    Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising and no tracking pixels on these pages.

    What is kept in your browser

    Two small flags are kept in your browser’s session storage: one remembers that you have already seen the intro animation, the other makes the page-to-page transition play. They never leave your device and disappear when you close the tab.

    The contact form

    The form does not send anything to a server. When you press send, your device opens your own email app with the message already written. What you send is then handled by your email provider and arrives in my inbox. I use it only to reply to you.

    Other companies’ servers

    To load, this site fetches fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries (GSAP, Three.js and Lenis) from public content delivery networks such as cdnjs and jsDelivr. Like any website request, this lets those services see your IP address and browser details. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs. Links to GitHub, YouTube and Instagram lead to services with their own privacy policies.

    Changes

    If I ever add analytics or anything else that collects data, I will update this page first.
